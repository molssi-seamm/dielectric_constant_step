dielectric_constant_step.dielectric_constant module#

Non-graphical part of the Dielectric Constant step in a SEAMM flowchart

The static dielectric constant from the fluctuations of the dipole of the cell in equilibrium MD with fixed charges and Ewald or PPPM electrostatics: ε = 1 + (⟨M²⟩ − ⟨M⟩²) / (3 V ε0 kB T). For an electrolyte, M is the dipole of the neutral molecules: the ions carry a current, not a bounded dipole. The analysis is in seamm_md_analysis.electric.

The MD is run before this step – in a Loop for independent runs – or read from files; the step analyzes it.

dielectric_constant_step.dielectric_constant.DEBYE = 4.80320471#

e·Å in debye
